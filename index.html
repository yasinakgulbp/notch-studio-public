BP96 STUDIO · Notch Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · Android package: com.bp96studio.notchstudio

Notch Studio is an Android utility by BP96 STUDIO. This policy explains access to notifications and device features, information stored on your device, and the optional weather request. Contact: bp96studio@gmail.com.

Notifications and media

If you grant Android notification access, the app reads notification titles, text, source app information and compatible active media sessions to display and control them in the island. These contents are not sent to BP96 STUDIO or the weather provider. The current notification queue is held in memory. Notification history is off by default; if enabled, the latest 50 entries are stored on your device and can be cleared in the app.

Optional accessibility service

Notch Studio uses the Android AccessibilityService API only as a display host to show its island above the status bar and on the lock screen. It is not a disability accessibility tool. The service does not retrieve window or screen contents, read typed text, simulate gestures, filter keys, perform actions for you, or send accessibility data. An in-app explanation and your explicit choice precede opening Android accessibility settings. You can skip or revoke this optional permission.

Device permissions

Display-over-other-apps permission shows the island outside the app. Phone-state permission provides incoming and ongoing call status; we do not read contacts, call logs or phone numbers and cannot place, answer or end calls. Nearby-devices permission provides Bluetooth connection information and a device name. Camera permission is used only to operate the flashlight; no camera images are captured. Notification permission is used for ongoing service and recording notifications. Permissions can be revoked in Android settings.

Audio and screen recordings

Microphone recording starts only when you select it and grant Android microphone permission. Screen recording requires Android's screen-sharing confirmation each time and does not capture audio. Recordings are stored in the app's private storage, limited to 1 hour or 100 MB per recording. You can stop, open, explicitly share/export or delete recordings. BP96 STUDIO does not upload recordings. When you choose a share destination, that destination handles the exported file under its own practices. Uninstalling the app deletes recordings you have not exported.

Optional weather service and information sent off device

Weather is optional. After the weather privacy prompt, tapping refresh sends the coordinates of the city or custom location you selected and your connection IP address to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) at api.met.no over HTTPS. GPS, background location and automatic background weather requests are not used. Coordinates are limited to four decimal places. These coordinates may describe a precise location you enter. MET Norway states that its access logs store request IP addresses and coordinates in its data centre in Oslo, Norway. This is not anonymous or ephemeral processing. Provider retention and data-subject requests are governed by MET Norway's terms and linked privacy statement. Do not use weather if you do not wish to send these data; the other tools remain available.

Weather results and your selected location are cached locally. Requests respect a minimum cache interval of 30 minutes and the provider's expiry time. Data are attributed to MET Norway under CC BY 4.0. No notification contents, recordings, phone or Bluetooth data are included in a weather request.

Apps, settings and local data

The app displays launchable installed apps so you can choose notification filters and delivery/sports notification sources. These lists and choices remain on your device. There is no account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, advertising identifier or BP96 STUDIO data server. The app does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Clear notification history and delete recordings from their screens. Settings, app selections and the weather cache remain locally until changed, app data are cleared, or the app is uninstalled. To remove all local data, use Android Settings → Apps → Notch Studio → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall. This does not delete copies you exported or MET Norway server logs. For weather request data, consult the provider's privacy statement or contact us for assistance; do not send notification contents, credentials or recordings in a support email unless necessary and intended.

Security, audience and changes

App-private storage and Android permission controls protect local information. Weather requests use HTTPS. No method guarantees absolute security. Notch Studio is a general phone utility, not a service designed specifically for children. This policy may be updated when data practices change; the effective date will be revised. Questions can be sent to bp96studio@gmail.com.
