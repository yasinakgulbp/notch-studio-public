Notch Studio — reviewer guide

BP96 STUDIO · com.bp96studio.notchstudio · No sign-in, payment or account is required.

  1. Open the app. English is selected by default. Continue through language selection, then continue or skip the introduction.
  2. The optional accessibility display has a separate explanation. You can skip it, return to the permission hub and reopen its explanation. Choosing Agree opens Android settings; enable Notch Studio island display there and return.
  3. Grant display-over-other-apps and notification access in their guides, then choose Get started. Enable the island from Home. Android displays an ongoing, silent notification with a Stop action.
  4. A notification expands on tap; horizontal swipes select queued messages, × dismisses a message, and tapping outside collapses the card. Media controls require a compatible player with an active MediaSession.
  5. Tools → Start audio recording requests microphone permission. Start recording again after granting permission. Stop and save to finish. Screen recording requires the Android screen-sharing approval on each attempt; it records video without audio.
  6. Weather is optional. Its privacy prompt explains that MET Norway receives and logs your selected coordinates and IP address. Cancelling this prompt makes no weather request.
